Pony Shape Editor

Click empty canvas to append a point to the selected layer. Drag a filled dot to move a point; drag its small hollow handle to bend the curve into it. Click a point to select it (edit exact numbers on the right, or press Delete/Backspace to remove it). Every part of the pony is editable, and the render-order list controls which draws in front of which. Use the tabs below to switch between the side view and the up/down (front/back) views — each has its own independent shapes and generates its own function.
