⛰️ Terrain

Empty clears a tile to nothing (no floor, no wall). Wall paints a real, stored solid tile by hand — nothing is ever added automatically. An empty tile bordered by floor is a valid Solidify target in-game (right-click a floor tile to carve one for a puzzle).

🖌️ Tool

Move chunk: drag a rectangle to lift everything inside it (floor, walls, objects, items, doors, runes) — it's cut immediately. Move the mouse to preview where it'll land, click to drop. Dropping stamps the whole rectangle: any gap in the selection wipes whatever was at the destination too, it doesn't just overlay on top. Right-click or Esc puts it back where it was.

🔍 View

Scroll to zoom. Middle-click + drag to pan. Right-click to erase under the cursor. Ctrl+Z to undo.

💧 Objects

Toggle start-frozen — click a placed crate to flag it as immobilized from the start of the level (needs Mirror+Frost to thaw in-game). Click again to unflag.

Toggle crackable wall — click a rock wall to flag it as crackable (Crack can mark it and a crate ramming it shatters it into floor). Every other wall is permanent, no matter how Crack is cast at it. Click again to unflag.

Plates are placed independently, one at a time. Use Pair below to decide which two work together, and Link to connect a pair to a lock.

🧩 Plate Groups

Group — click a lone plate to add it to the active group below (starts one automatically if none is active yet); click a plate already in it to remove it again; click a plate from a different group to switch to that one instead. Link lock — click a lock to link/unlink it with the active group.

✨ Items

A single collectible concept — click "+ Item" then click/drag on the map to place, its zone inferred from the floor beneath it. What guards it (a nature gate, a puzzle...) is just whatever you build around it.
